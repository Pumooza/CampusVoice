Executive Summary</strong></h1><p>CampusVoice is an official university complaint and discussion platform that enables students to safely report issues—publicly or privately—while maintaining institutional accountability through a trusted Student Representative oversight system.</p><p>The platform addresses the most critical barrier to campus feedback: student fear of retaliation. By combining moderated anonymity with role-based access control, CampusVoice allows students to speak freely while ensuring all complaints are properly reviewed, tracked, and resolved.</p><h1><strong>2. Problem Statement</strong></h1><h2><strong>2.1 Core Problem</strong></h2><p>Students at universities often fail to report legitimate issues due to fear of retaliation from faculty, administration, or peers. Existing feedback channels are either informal (verbal), untracked, or expose the complainant's identity by default.</p><h2><strong>2.2 Pain Points</strong></h2><ul><li>No safe, structured channel for student complaints</li><li>Fear of identity exposure when reporting teacher misconduct</li><li>Complaints fall through the cracks with no accountability or tracking</li><li>No transparency around complaint resolution timelines</li><li>Teachers can ignore complaints with no oversight mechanism</li></ul><h2><strong>2.3 Success Criteria</strong></h2><ul><li>Students freely submit complaints without fear of retaliation</li><li>All complaints are reviewed within 48 hours</li><li>All complaints are resolved within 7 days</li><li>KPI: Total number of solved complaints increases over time</li></ul><h1><strong>3. Product Vision &amp; Goals</strong></h1><h2><strong>3.1 Vision Statement</strong></h2><p>CampusVoice will be the trusted, official voice of the student body — a platform that feels modern and student-friendly while maintaining the gravitas of institutional accountability.</p><h2><strong>3.2 Design Philosophy</strong></h2><ul><li>Moderated Anonymity: Not fully anonymous. Teachers cannot identify anonymous students. Student Representatives can identify any student for accountability purposes.</li><li>Discussion-Oriented: Complaints function as discussions, not just tickets.</li><li>Social Feed: Public complaints are displayed in a social-media-style feed.</li><li>Official Branding: The platform must feel authoritative and trustworthy, not casual.</li></ul><h1><strong>4. Descriptions</strong></h2><h3><strong>Student</strong></h3><ul><li>Submits complaints publicly or privately</li><li>Can choose anonymous or named submission</li><li>Can participate in their own complaint discussions</li><li>Can reopen a solved complaint</li><li>Can track all their complaints via personal dashboard</li><li>Cannot comment on other students' complaints</li></ul><h3><strong>Teacher / Department Head</strong></h3><ul><li>Teachers and Department Heads share identical permissions</li><li>Can reply to and update status of complaints directed at them</li><li>Can report abusive complaints to the Representative</li><li>Cannot delete complaints or view anonymous identities</li><li>Self-register but require Representative approval for role activation</li></ul><h3><strong>Student Representative (Super Admin)</strong></h3><ul><li>Full oversight of the entire platform</li><li>Can view the identity behind any anonymous complaint</li><li>Can approve/reject new user registrations and assign roles</li><li>Can ban users permanently (no appeals, no temporary bans)</li><li>Can delete, hide, and moderate any complaint</li><li>Has access to analytics dashboard</li><li>Cannot edit complaint content or impersonate users</li><li>Account is manually created (no self-registration)</li></ul><h3><strong>Alumni</strong></h3><ul><li>Same access as students (can register and submit complaints)</li></ul><h1><strong>5. Complaint System</strong></h1><h2><strong>5.1 Complaint Types</strong></h2><table><tr><td><p><strong>Visibility</strong></p></td><td><p><strong>Who Can See It</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Public</strong></p></td><td><p>All teachers + Student Representative + all students (social feed)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Private</strong></p></td><td><p>Selected teacher(s) + Student Representative only</p></td></tr></table><h2><strong>5.2 Complaint Categories</strong></h2><p>Each complaint must be assigned one or more categories from a dropdown. Additional free-form tags can be added.</p><ul><li>Academic</li><li>Teacher Behavior</li><li>Harassment</li><li>Attendance</li><li>Hostel</li><li>Fee Issues</li><li>Exam / Result Issues</li><li>IT / Lab Issues</li><li>University Issues</li></ul><h2><strong>5.3 Complaint Fields</strong></h2><ul><li>Summary (short field) — required</li><li>Description — max 1,000 words, required</li><li>Category — dropdown, multi-select, required</li><li>Tags — free-form tagging, optional</li><li>Target Teacher(s) / Department(s) — searchable, multi-select</li><li>Visibility — Public or Private</li><li>Identity — Named or Anonymous</li><li>Attachments — up to 2 MB, PDF / JPG / PNG only</li></ul><h2><strong>5.4 Attachment Rules</strong></h2><ul><li>Allowed formats: PDF, JPG, PNG</li><li>Maximum file size: 2 MB per file</li><li>File scanning: Not required for Version 1</li><li>File naming: Filenames are automatically renamed to randomized strings (e.g., evidence_91af2.png) to prevent accidental identity exposure</li></ul><h2><strong>5.5 Anonymity Model</strong></h2><ul><li>Anonymous complaints are treated equally to named complaints</li><li>There is no limit on how many anonymous complaints a student can submit</li><li>Teachers CANNOT view anonymous identities under any circumstance</li><li>Student Representatives CAN view anonymous identities for accountability purposes</li><li>Students can track their own anonymous complaints via their personal dashboard</li><li>Anonymous students can continue replying anonymously in discussion threads</li></ul><h2><strong>5.6 Complaint Status Lifecycle</strong></h2><table><tr><td><p><strong>Status</strong></p></td><td><p><strong>Description</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Pending</strong></p></td><td><p>Complaint submitted, awaiting initial review by a teacher or representative</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Under Review</strong></p></td><td><p>A teacher or representative has acknowledged and is reviewing the complaint</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Awaiting Student Response</strong></p></td><td><p>Teacher or representative needs additional information from the student</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Solved</strong></p></td><td><p>Complaint has been resolved and marked closed. Removed from public feed. Student can reopen.</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Rejected</strong></p></td><td><p>Complaint was rejected. Private rejections become public temporarily and are deleted after 7 days.</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Hidden</strong></p></td><td><p>Complaint hidden by representative moderation. Not visible on public feed.</p></td></tr></table><h2><strong>5.7 Complaint Lifecycle Rules</strong></h2><ul><li>Response SLA: All complaints must be reviewed within 48 hours</li><li>Resolution SLA: All complaints must be resolved within 7 days</li><li>Solved complaints are removed from the public feed</li><li>Students can reopen solved complaints (no additional workflow defined)</li><li>Rejected private complaints become public temporarily and are permanently deleted after 7 days</li><li>Ignored complaints remain open; the Student Representative can intervene</li><li>There is no escalation workflow in Version 1</li><li>Complaints are permanently deleted after 1 month of storage</li><li>Complaint deletion is permanent (no soft-delete)</li></ul><h1><strong>6. Discussion &amp; Communication</strong></h1><h2><strong>6.1 Discussion Model</strong></h2><p>All communication between students, teachers, and representatives happens exclusively through complaint discussion threads. No direct messaging between users exists.</p><ul><li>Communication is two-way (students and teachers can both reply)</li><li>Anonymous students can continue replying anonymously within the thread</li><li>Teachers can reply to anonymous complaints without seeing the identity</li><li>Student Representatives can join any discussion thread</li><li>Replies can include attachments (PDF, JPG, PNG, 2 MB limit)</li></ul><h2><strong>6.2 Comment Restrictions</strong></h2><ul><li>Students CANNOT comment on other students' complaints</li><li>No upvotes, reactions, or support indicators</li><li>No direct messaging between any users</li></ul><h1><strong>7. User Registration &amp; Verification</strong></h1><h2><strong>7.1 Registration Flow</strong></h2><ul><li>Students, Teachers, Alumni: self-register on the platform</li><li>All accounts require manual approval by the Student Representative before activation</li><li>University email verification is NOT required</li><li>Two-factor authentication is NOT required</li><li>The Student Representative account is manually created by system admin</li><li>Representatives CANNOT create teacher accounts (teachers self-register)</li><li>Representatives CANNOT reset user passwords</li></ul><h2><strong>7.2 Account Rules</strong></h2><ul><li>One account per person; no dual roles</li><li>Suspended students lose all complaint access</li><li>Alumni have the same access level as active students</li></ul><h2><strong>7.3 Banning</strong></h2><ul><li>Bans are permanent — no temporary bans</li><li>No ban appeals</li><li>No moderation history is kept</li><li>Triggers: fake complaints, abuse, spam, harassment, or representative discretion</li></ul><h1><strong>8. Notifications</strong></h1><h2><strong>8.1 Notification Types</strong></h2><ul><li>Complaint received (for teachers/representative)</li><li>Status changed (for complaint submitter)</li><li>Reply added (for relevant parties in the thread)</li></ul><h2><strong>8.2 Delivery Channel</strong></h2><ul><li>Web app notifications only</li><li>No email, SMS, or push notification support in Version 1</li><li>Students see status changes only — not detailed resolution notes</li><li>No automated reminders for unresolved complaints</li></ul><h1><strong>9. Dashboard Specifications</strong></h1><h2><strong>9.1 Student Dashboard</strong></h2><ul><li>Home Feed — public complaints in social-feed format</li><li>Submit Complaint — complaint submission form</li><li>My Complaints — personal complaint history (incl. anonymous)</li><li>Departments — department listing and filtering</li><li>Profile — account settings and management</li></ul><h2><strong>9.2 Teacher / Department Head Dashboard</strong></h2><ul><li>Assigned Complaints — private complaints directed at this teacher</li><li>Public Complaints — all public complaints, filterable</li><li>Complaint Details — individual complaint view with thread</li><li>Report Complaint — flag complaints as abusive to representative</li><li>Categories — browse by category</li></ul><h2><strong>9.3 Student Representative Dashboard</strong></h2><ul><li>Pending Registrations — approve or reject new accounts</li><li>Role Management — assign and manage user roles</li><li>Complaint Moderation — view, hide, delete complaints</li><li>Hidden Complaints — view all currently hidden complaints</li><li>Reported Complaints — review complaints flagged by teachers</li><li>Ban Management — manage banned users</li><li>Public Feed Oversight — monitor and manage the public complaint feed</li><li>System Statistics — analytics module (see Section 10)</li></ul><h1><strong>10. Public Feed</strong></h1><h2><strong>10.1 Feed Behavior</strong></h2><ul><li>Newest-first ordering</li><li>Displays only active (non-solved, non-hidden) public complaints</li><li>Solved complaints are automatically removed from the feed</li><li>Representatives can hide any complaint from the feed</li></ul><h2><strong>10.2 Feed Features</strong></h2><ul><li>Category filtering</li><li>Department filtering</li><li>Teacher filtering</li><li>Status filtering</li><li>Date filtering</li><li>Status badges displayed on each complaint card</li><li>Pinned announcements (optional, representative-controlled)</li></ul><h2><strong>10.3 Feed Restrictions</strong></h2><ul><li>No search bar — filtering only</li><li>No upvotes or reactions</li><li>Students cannot comment on others' complaints</li><li>No transparency/public dashboard</li></ul><h1><strong>11. Analytics &amp; Reporting</strong></h1><h2><strong>11.1 Version 1 Scope</strong></h2><p>Analytics are available exclusively to the Student Representative. No public transparency dashboard. No export functionality.</p><h2><strong>11.2 Analytics Modules</strong></h2><ul><li>System Statistics (total complaints, resolved, pending, rejected)</li><li>Complaint volume by category</li><li>Complaint volume by department</li><li>Resolution rate and average resolution time</li><li>All analytics hide anonymous identities</li></ul><p>Note: No formal reports, no data export, and no performance dashboards exist in Version 1.</p><h1><strong>12. Search &amp; Filtering</strong></h1><p>There is no free-text search on complaints in Version 1. All discovery is through categorical filters.</p><h2><strong>12.1 Available Filters</strong></h2><ul><li>Department</li><li>Category</li><li>Teacher</li><li>Status</li><li>Date</li></ul><p>Students cannot search the public complaint feed directly.</p><h1><strong>13. Technical Specifications</strong></h1><h2><strong>13.1 Recommended Tech Stack</strong></h2><table><tr><td><p><strong>Layer</strong></p></td><td><p><strong>Technology</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Frontend</strong></p></td><td><p>React.js + Tailwind CSS + Shadcn/UI</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Backend</strong></p></td><td><p>Node.js + Express.js</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Database</strong></p></td><td><p>PostgreSQL</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>ORM</strong></p></td><td><p>Prisma</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Authentication</strong></p></td><td><p>JWT (JSON Web Tokens)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>File Storage</strong></p></td><td><p>To be decided by developer</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Hosting</strong></p></td><td><p>DigitalOcean VPS</p></td></tr><tr><td><p><strong>Architecture</strong></p></td><td><p>To be decided by developer</p></td></tr></table><h2><strong>13.2 Scale Requirements</strong></h2><ul><li>Expected concurrent users: 500</li><li>Single university deployment (not multi-tenant)</li><li>No mobile API support in Version 1</li><li>No CI/CD pipeline or automated testing in Version 1</li></ul><h2><strong>13.3 Security &amp; Privacy</strong></h2><ul><li>No audit logs in Version 1</li><li>No IP/device logging</li><li>No 2FA for any account type</li><li>File uploads renamed to randomized strings to prevent identity exposure</li><li>Anonymous complaint identities stored securely; accessible only to Representative</li><li>Permanent deletion only (no soft-delete)</li></ul><h2><strong>13.4 Data Retention</strong></h2><ul><li>Complaints stored for 1 month then permanently deleted</li><li>Complaint data is owned by the university administration / Student Representative governance</li></ul><h1><strong>14. MVP Scope vs Future Versions</strong></h1><h2><strong>14.1 Version 1 — Must Have</strong></h2><ul><li>Anonymous complaint submission</li><li>Public and private complaint visibility modes</li><li>Student Representative oversight and identity access</li><li>Teacher replies and complaint status management</li><li>Complaint status lifecycle (Pending → Under Review → Solved / Rejected)</li><li>Manual user approval and role management</li><li>Complaint discussion threads</li><li>File attachments (PDF, JPG, PNG, 2 MB)</li><li>In-app notifications</li><li>Student, Teacher, and Representative dashboards</li><li>Public complaint feed with filtering</li><li>Analytics for Representative</li><li>48-hour review SLA and 7-day resolution SLA</li></ul><h2><strong>14.2 Explicitly Out of Scope for Version 1</strong></h2><ul><li>Complaint export / download functionality</li><li>Email or push notifications</li><li>Mobile application</li><li>Search (text-based)</li><li>Formal reporting module</li><li>Complaint escalation workflow</li><li>Two-factor authentication</li><li>Multi-university support</li><li>CI/CD or automated testing</li></ul><h2><strong>14.3 Future Roadmap</strong></h2><ul><li>Version 2: Mobile application (iOS + Android)</li><li>Version 2: Complaint export system</li><li>Version 2: Download functionality</li></ul><h1><strong>15. Non-Functional Requirements</strong></h1><h2><strong>15.1 Performance</strong></h2><ul><li>Page load time &lt; 3 seconds under normal load</li><li>Support 500 concurrent users without degradation</li></ul><h2><strong>15.2 Usability</strong></h2><ul><li>Desktop-first design (primary breakpoint)</li><li>English language UI only (text inputs accept any language)</li><li>No dark mode</li><li>Modern, student-friendly design with official institutional feel</li></ul><h2><strong>15.3 Reliability</strong></h2><ul><li>Platform must be accessible during university operating hours</li><li>Complaint data persisted reliably; no loss from server restarts</li></ul><h2><strong>15.4 Maintainability</strong></h2><ul><li>Single developer codebase — code must be well-documented</li><li>Developer retains architectural decisions (hosting, file storage)</li></ul><h1><strong>16.
